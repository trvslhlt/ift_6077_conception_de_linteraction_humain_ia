Write a two-page research proposal using the 2-column [ACM SIGCHI format](https://chi2025.acm.org/chi-publication-formats/). This proposal should include:

* An Abstract. This should be written like the abstract of a research article, minus the results. Typically, this would follow a structure like this:  
  * 1 to 2 sentences of background. This should should help provide the context and motivation for the work. You can refer to previous work in the area if you are building on top of an existing work.  
  * 1 to 2 sentences explaining the big idea.  
  * 1 to 3 sentences providing details on your solution. For example, you can explain in more details how the system will work and how users will interact with it. Here, you can also refer to some of the interaction theories seen in class.  
  * (optional) 0 to 1 sentence statement about the impact of this work. What do we learn from it? How will others be able to build upon it? Why does it matter? Who is it helping?  
* An Introduction that:  
  * Motivates the problem and explain why it is important (typically 1 to 2 paragraphs, depending on how much context needs to be provided to introduce the problem)  
  * Explains why existing solutions are not enough. You could mention 1 to 3 of the most similar works. However, do not just copy the related work section. If anything, this should be a super short summary of your related work section. Or you could decide to keep this explanation at a "higher-level" and avoid mentioning specific works.  
  * Describes your solution. Here, I expect that you will refer to one or multiple of the theories seen in-class. I realize that, by the time you are writing the proposal, some theories have not been discussed in-class yet. I would recommend  for you to skim future theories to see if they might be relevant and inspiring to you. It is OK to mention a theory we have not seen yet. It is also OK to be a little vague if you have not figured out the solution yet.  
    * To help describe your solution you can include a sketch of your system and refer to it in the introduction. This figure can be a photo of a drawing on a whiteboard, as long as it is clear.  
* A Related Work section that provides the necessary background to understand your work and situate your work within the literature. Typically, you will want to group previous work based on themes, forming 2 to 4 sub-sections. When mentioning previous works, make sure to explain in what way your work is different, or in what ways your work builds on top of these previous works.

Note that this structure is a recommendation, but you are free to adapt it, as long as all the points are covered. Please upload a PDF file.
